# Before submitting to Moodle

The assignment requires one ZIP containing:

1. Complete Android Studio project source code.
2. Your 5–7 minute narrated demonstration video (MP4).
3. Your written report (DOCX or PDF) with real screenshots from your running app and your GitHub repository link.

Also make sure:
- Your GitHub repository is public, or your lecturer is added as a collaborator if private.
- You have at least 10 meaningful commits showing development over time.
- Your report uses Times New Roman 12 pt and 1.5 line spacing, with Harvard referencing as required by the brief.
- Replace the example/placeholder student details with your own.
- Test Create, Read, Update and Delete.
- Test the strict recipe matching rule by adding/removing an ingredient.
- Close and reopen the app to demonstrate persistence.
- Keep the final ZIP under 50 MB.
